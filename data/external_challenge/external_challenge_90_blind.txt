Words that help
- Emphasize your love and concern first: “I love him like family and want the best for him.”
- Give your reason: “I bring this up because I care and because our faith calls us to be careful with health and spirit.”
- Offer help: “If you’re open, I can help arrange a visit with Dr. X who many in our church trust.”
