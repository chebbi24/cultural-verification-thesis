\chapter{Operational Framework for Cultural Appropriateness}
\label{chap:framework}

\section{Purpose}
A single label such as culturally appropriate is difficult to interpret. Vericult therefore divides the task into ten operational dimensions. They are categories for the verifier, not a universal theory of culture.

\section{Scoring}
Each applicable dimension receives 0, 1, 2, or abstain. A score of 0 means material misalignment, 1 means partial or limited appropriateness, and 2 means appropriate handling. Abstention means that a defensible score cannot be established.

\section{Tendency, Rule, and Value}
A cultural tendency is not a binding rule. A social norm may vary by context. A legal or institutional rule has a formal source. A value judgment can remain contested even when one view is common. Keeping these categories separate helps reduce cultural essentialism.

\section{Limitations}
The dimensions overlap and reflect researcher design choices. A workplace greeting can involve both D02 and D08, while a historical symbol can involve D09 and D10. Construct validity must therefore be discussed separately from software implementation.
