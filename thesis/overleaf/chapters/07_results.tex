\section{Assessability}

Two outcomes occur before normal cultural scoring: \texttt{not\_culturally\_applicable} and \texttt{not\_assessable}.

The culturally applicable count is:

\begin{equation}
N_{applicable} = N_{completed} - N_{not_culturally_applicable}.
\end{equation}

Among culturally applicable prompts, the substantive assessability rate is:

\begin{equation}
A = \frac{N_{applicable} - N_{not_assessable}}{N_{applicable}}.
\end{equation}
